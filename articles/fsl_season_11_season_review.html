Season 11 Full Season Review

From GSL groups to a Seed 4 title run

Season 11 used the GSL-style group into a ladder playoff, with Friday 7 PM ET and/or Saturday 12 PM ET windows. Matchups stayed near-level via live power rankings. PulledTheBoys took Seed 1 — then Special Tactics climbed the entire elimination ladder and won the Grand Finals 14–4.

Compiled from: fsl_schedule, fsl_matches, rankings.json ((prod snapshot), and the mid-playoff review baseline.

Scoring basis: map points (1v1, Ace, 2v2). A 1–1 Bo2 is a split — one map each, no sweep.

03 • Team notes

Four stories, one trophy

Respectful framing — every roster moved the season.

Special Tactics

Seed 4 • Champions via the long path
3–2 series • 39–30 maps
DarkMenaceHyperTurtleReevousOsSirMalagantCubano

Special Tactics wrote the Season 11 story. Seeded fourth after a one-map losers-bracket Ace, they beat ASH in elimination, edged POG on a SirMalagant Ace, then blanked PTB 14–4 in the Grand Finals.

DarkMenace #3 · G1 finally got a full sample and owned the big moments. HyperTurtle, Reevou, sOs, and Cubano turned “dangerous small sample” into a title roster.

Legacy: From the losers bracket to champions — the deepest ladder run the format can produce.

PulledTheBoys

Seed 1 • Group kings, Finals short night
2–1 series • 25–27 maps
NeutrophilLittleReaperChienPwnDarkbladeMvonLipwig

PulledTheBoys looked like four-peat favorites: 2–0 through the winners bracket, Seed 1, and a Finals bye. Group depth from LittleReaper, ChienPwn, and Neutrophil was real.

The Finals were a shock. Cubano and DarkMenace took bites out of Neutrophil, sOs swept Darkblade, and HyperTurtle closed on MvonLipwig.

Frame: A historically strong group stage does not guarantee a Finals night. PTB still set the regular-bracket standard.

PSIOP Gaming

Seed 2 • Ace specialists
2–2 series • 27–30 maps
MedicJRCrankytoasterBeanTheBardJmpzHurtnTime

PSIOP Gaming lived on the Ace — Week 4 and Week 5 both needed closers (BeanTheBard, Crankytoaster). That grit earned Seed 2.

In the Finals berth match, MedicJR #2 · G1 went nuclear with sweeps of DarkMenace and Cubano, but SirMalagant answered the Ace against Jmpz and ST moved on.

PSIOP’s season is the template for “record understates competitiveness.”

Angry Space Hares

Seed 3 • Star power, early exit
1–3 series • 25–29 maps
Chat-OmicSunnyDFRostGreatArchonPacha

Angry Space Hares owned Opening Week 1 and boasted the season’s loudest individual lines in SunnyD and Chat-Omic #1 · G1. The problem was conversion later.

After seeding and elimination losses to POG and ST, the Hares’ year ended one map short in Week 6 — FRost nearly stole it before DarkMenace closed the Ace.

Top-end talent was never the question; converting elimination nights was.
